Triggers

The microinteraction begins when the user hovers their mouse over the heart icon. The cursor changes from the standard arrow to a pointer, indicating that the heart is clickable. Clicking the heart adds the product to Favorites. Clicking the heart again removes the product from Favorites.

Rules

When the heart is not selected, it appears as a blue outline with an unfilled center. When the user clicks the heart, the selected state is activated and the heart fills blue. If the user clicks the heart again, the selected state is removed and the heart returns to its original outlined appearance.

Feedback

When the user's cursor is over the heart, the cursor changes into a pointer and the heart responds with a subtle hover animation. When clicked, the heart quickly shrinks and expands to create a bounce or pop effect while changing from an outlined heart to a solid blue heart. When deselected, the heart animates again and returns to its outlined state.

Loops & Modes

The heart has two modes: favorited and unfavorited. The user can repeatedly switch between these modes by clicking the heart. Each click updates the appearance of the icon and repeats the corresponding animation.
