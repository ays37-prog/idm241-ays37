Marshalls - Leather And Suede Waterproof Celeste Mid Snow Boots

Upon opening the Marshalls shopping link to the example product, snowboots, you will find the "Favorities" icon. This heart shaped icon is located in the center of the screen, between the product image and the description. It is displayed as a blue outline with an empty (while) fill.

Object: "Favorite" Icon

-- LOCATION

  • You will find the "Favorite" icon upon opening the Marshalls product page for the snow boots.
  • The heart icon is located near the center of the page, in between the photo of the snow boots and the product description.
  • In its default state (unfavorited) the icon has a blue outline with a white (empty) interior.

TRIGGERS

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF
  • Click ON
  • Click OFF

RULES

-- Hover ON

  • Cursor changes from an arrow to a pointer hand.
  • Product URL preview appears in the bottom left corner of the browser in small grey text.

-- Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes from the pointer hand back to the default arrow.
  • Product URL preview disappears from the bottom left corner of the browser.

-- Click ON

  • Product changes from unfavorited to favorited in the database.
  • Heart icon changes from the white (empty) fill to blue fill.
  • Product heart undergoes one bounce animation, growing then shrinking before returning to its original size.
  • Favorites heart icon in the top navigation bar also changes from white (empty) fill to blue fill.
  • Favorites heart icon in the top navigation also performs one bounce animation, growing then shrinking before returning to its original size.
  • Favorites counter increases by 1 (0 to 1).
  • A confirmation pop up notification appears near the Favorites area under the top navigation bar.
  • This confirmation notification contains a blue heart icon and the text “Saved!", giving the user more proof that their action worked successfully.
  • Snowboots is added to the user's saved Favorites.

-- Click OFF

  • Product changes from favorited to unfavorited in the database.
  • Product heart changes from the blue fill to white (empty).
  • Product heart undergoes one bounce animation, growing then shrinking before returning to its original size.
  • Favorites heart icon in the top navigation bar also changes from blue fill to white (empty) fill.
  • Favorites heart in the top navigation performs one bounce animation, growing then shrinking before returning to its original size.
  • Favorites counter decreases by 1 (1 to 0).
  • Confirmation pop up notification dissapears from the Favorites area under the top navigation bar.
  • This confirmation notification contains a blue heart icon and the text “Saved!", giving the user more proof that their action worked successfully.
  • Snowboots is removed from the user's saved Favorites.

FEEDBACK

-- Hover ON

  • Cursor visually changes from an arrow to a pointer hand.
  • Snowboots URL preview becomes visible in the lower-left corner of the browser.

-- Hover OFF

  • Cursor visually changes from a pointer hand back to an arrow.
  • Snowboots URL preview disappears.

-- Click ON

  • Product heart fills in with blue.
  • Product heart visibly grows and shrinks in one bounce animation.
  • Favorites heart in the top navigation fills blue.
  • Favorites heart performs the corresponding bounce animation.
  • Favorites count visibly increases from 0 to 1.
  • “Saved!” pop up notification appears beneath the Favorites area with a blue heart icon.
  • No audible feedback is produced.
  • No haptic feedback occurs.

- Click OFF

  • Product heart returns to its blue outlined state.
  • Product heart visibly grows and shrinks in one bounce animation.
  • Favorites heart in the top navigation returns to its default state (blue border, white fill).
  • Favorites heart performs a corresponding bounce animation.
  • Favorites count visibly decreases from 1 to 0.
  • “Saved!” pop up notification disappears.
  • No audible feedback is produced.
  • No haptic feedback occurs on desktop.

LOOPS AND MODES

  • Favorite heart is the toggle between two states: unfavorited (default) and favorited.
  • Clicking the heart when it's unfavorited changes the snowboots to the favorited state in the database.
  • Clicking the heart when it's favorited returns the snowboots to the unfavorited state in the database.
  • The favorite/unfavorite interaction can reoccur every time the user clicks the heart.
  • Favorites count relies on the real user's current number of saved products, rather than always specifically changing from 0 and 1.
  • Favorites heart in the top navigation bar reflects the overall Favorites state rather than only the state of this individual product.
  • “Saved!” pop up notification depends on if the product is in its favorited state.
